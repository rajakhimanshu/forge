### ANALYSIS RULES
1. **Uniqueness (Moat)**: How hard is this to copy? If it relies on a "Network Effect," score it high (7+).
2. **Market Gap**: Is there a real-world friction being solved? If the current solution is fragmented, manual, or painful, the gap is HIGH (8+).
3. **Feasibility**: Can a small team build the MVP in 4-8 weeks?
4. **Timing**: Why is AI making this possible *now* when it wasn't possible 3 years ago?

### CRITICAL INSTRUCTION FOR LOCAL & NICHE PROJECTS
Compare the idea to the **actual alternatives users currently use** — not to irrelevant global giants. For example:
- A hyper-local community tool should be compared to WhatsApp groups or notice boards, not Facebook.
- A developer productivity tool should be compared to shell scripts or spreadsheets, not GitHub Enterprise.
- A small-business tool should be compared to manual processes or local workarounds, not Salesforce.

If the local/niche alternative is poor or fragmented, the Market Gap is HIGH.
